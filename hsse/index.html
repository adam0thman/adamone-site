Health, Safety, Security and Environment (HSSE) Policy

Document AOS-HSSE-POL-001 Approved by the Managing Director, 2021 Reviewed yearly · last reviewed 14 November 2026 Owner: Managing Director

adamOne Services (AOS) provides SAP technical services: BASIS administration and managed operations, ABAP development, BTP extensions and custom Fiori apps. We will not carry out any work that we cannot do safely, securely and without harm to the environment. No business objective takes priority over this.

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, visitors and anyone working on our behalf, at our own premises, at customer sites and when working remotely on customer systems.

Our commitments

We will:

  1. Prevent injury and ill health by providing safe workplaces, safe systems of work, and the information, training and supervision people need.
  2. Comply with the law — the Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994, the Environmental Quality Act 1974, the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, the Employment Act 1955 and every other legal and customer HSSE requirement that applies to our work.
  3. Manage risk by identifying hazards, assessing risks and putting controls in place before work starts, and reviewing them when the work changes.
  4. Protect people, assets and information, including our customers' systems and data, from loss, theft, misuse and cyber attack.
  5. Protect the environment by preventing pollution, using energy efficiently and disposing of electronic and scheduled waste through licensed channels.
  6. Look after our people's welfare, including their mental health, and maintain a workplace free from harassment, forced labour and substance misuse.
  7. Be ready for emergencies with tested plans for injury, fire, security breach, cyber incident and loss of service.
  8. Consult our employees and contractors on HSSE matters and act on what they tell us.
  9. Report and learn by reporting every incident and near miss, investigating the cause and sharing the lessons.
  10. Improve continually by setting measurable HSSE objectives, auditing our performance and reviewing this policy at least once a year.

Everyone's authority and duty

  • Stop work. Anyone may stop any work they believe is unsafe or insecure. No one will be penalised for doing so in good faith.
  • Report. Everyone must report hazards, incidents, near misses and security concerns immediately.
  • Follow the rules. Everyone must follow our HSSE procedures and those of the customer whose site or system they are working on.

Accountability

The Managing Director is accountable for this policy. Line managers are responsible for implementing it in their teams. Our HSSE Management System sets out how it is carried out.

This policy is published on our website, communicated to everyone who works for or with us, and reviewed at least once a year. To raise an HSSE or security concern, or to ask about our HSSE Management System, write to hello@adamoneservices.com.

This policy was approved by the Managing Director, adamOne Services (AOS), in 2021. It is reviewed every year and was last reviewed on 14 November 2026.
